# APU Tuition Reduction Scholarship for International Students

## Key facts
APU Tuition Reduction Scholarship for International Students is offered by Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University in Japan. Level: undergraduate. Funding: tuition reduction of 30%, 50%, 65%, 80% or 100% for the standard period of study. Application deadline: not specified. Status: not specified. Official page: 
https://admissions.apu.ac.jp/costs_scholarships/before_enrollment/?lang=en.

## Eligibility
APU Tuition Reduction Scholarship for International Students is for international applicants applying for admission before enrolment and meeting the University's student-residence requirements in Japan. Selection considers academic ability and financial need. GPA and English/Japanese language requirements depend on the admission route; scholarship-wide thresholds: not specified.

## Required documents
APU Tuition Reduction Scholarship for International Students requires an APU admission application, academic records, proof of language proficiency and financial information as required by the admission and scholarship process. The exact checklist depends on the application route.
