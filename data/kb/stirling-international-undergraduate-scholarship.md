# International Undergraduate Scholarship

## Key facts
International Undergraduate Scholarship is offered by the University of Stirling to eligible international undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: GBP 2,500 per year, up to GBP 10,000 over four years, deducted from annual tuition fees. Eligible start dates (intake or entry): January 2027, September 2027 or January 2028. Application method: Assessment is automatic. Application deadline: not specified. Status: not specified. Official page: 
https://www.stir.ac.uk/scholarships/general/undergraduate/international-undergraduate-scholarship/.

## Eligibility
International Undergraduate Scholarship is for international-fee students holding a conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible full-time undergraduate course starting in January 2027, September 2027 or January 2028. Assessment is automatic. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
International Undergraduate Scholarship does not require a separate scholarship application according to the overview. Applicants need an eligible undergraduate course application and offer. Additional documents follow the course admission requirements.
