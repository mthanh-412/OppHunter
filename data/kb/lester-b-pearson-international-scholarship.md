# Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship

## Key facts
Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship is awarded by the University of Toronto to outstanding international students entering their first undergraduate degree. Level: undergraduate. Funding: tuition, books, incidental fees and residence support for four years. Application deadline: 2026-11-06. Status: closed. Official page: 
https://future.utoronto.ca/pearson-scholarships.

## Eligibility
Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship applicants must be international students in their final year of secondary school in 2026/27 or have graduated no earlier than June 2026, and must begin at the University of Toronto in September 2027. A school nomination is required. Students already attending a post-secondary institution are ineligible. GPA and IELTS scholarship thresholds: not specified; admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship requires a school nomination, an undergraduate admission application to the University of Toronto and a separate Pearson Scholarship application. Supporting academic records and other materials must follow the official application instructions.
