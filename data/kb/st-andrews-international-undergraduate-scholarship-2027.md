# International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of St Andrews to overseas-fee undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: annual tuition-fee support assessed according to financial need. Application deadline: 2027-01-27. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/study/scholarships/scholarships-catalogue/undergraduate-scholarships/international-undergraduate-scholarship/.

## Eligibility
International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 is for overseas-fee students starting an undergraduate degree in 2027. Selection is based on financial need and the applicant's circumstances. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 requires an undergraduate application to the University of St Andrews and the relevant international scholarship application. Applicants should provide information and evidence of financial need as requested.
