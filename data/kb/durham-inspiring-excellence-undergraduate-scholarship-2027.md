# Inspiring Excellence Undergraduate Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Inspiring Excellence Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 is offered by Durham University to eligible international undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: up to GBP 15,000 for a three-year programme or GBP 30,000 for a longer eligible programme, paid in annual instalments subject to the award terms. Application deadline: 2026-12-07. Status: open. Official page: 
https://dur.ac.uk/study/scholarships/international/durham-inspiring-excellence-scholarships/undergraduate/.

## Eligibility
Inspiring Excellence Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 is for international/overseas-fee applicants applying for an eligible undergraduate course starting in September 2027. Applicants need a valid UCAS ID. Academic achievement and potential are assessed. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS minimums: not specified; course entry requirements apply.

## Required documents
Inspiring Excellence Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 requires a UCAS course application and UCAS ID, followed by the online scholarship application. Applicants should provide the requested statements about achievements, potential and expected impact. Additional evidence must follow the official terms.
