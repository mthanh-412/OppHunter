# KLE Companion Scholarship 2026

## Key facts
KLE Companion Scholarship 2026 is an award from the KLE Mentoring Program for Vietnamese university students in their first or second year. Level: undergraduate. Funding: 18 ACCA scholarships, including 3 awards of up to 100%, 5 of up to 60%, and 10 of up to 50%, with mentoring and career support. Application deadline: 2026-10-10. Status: closed. Official page: 
https://kle.edu.vn/hoc-bong-dong-hanh-2026-cung-ban-tu-nhung-nam-dau-dai-hoc-den-hanh-trinh-su-nghiep/.

## Eligibility
KLE Companion Scholarship 2026 is open to Vietnamese university students in Year 1 or Year 2 who are interested in Accounting, Auditing or Finance. GPA requirement: not specified. IELTS requirement: not specified. Applicants should demonstrate motivation and potential for a career in the relevant fields.

## Required documents
KLE Companion Scholarship 2026 requires an application form and information about the applicant's profile, experience and goals. A complete supporting-document checklist: not specified.
