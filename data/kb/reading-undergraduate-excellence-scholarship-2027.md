# Undergraduate Excellence Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Undergraduate Excellence Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of Reading to eligible international undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: GBP 5,000 reduction in tuition fees for each eligible year of study. Application deadline: 2026-12-31. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.reading.ac.uk/ready-to-study/study/Fees-and-funding/scholarships/undergraduate-excellence-scholarship-2027.

## Eligibility
Undergraduate Excellence Scholarship 2027 is for international-fee students holding an offer for an eligible undergraduate course, including an integrated master's course, for 2027/28 entry. Academic merit is assessed. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
Undergraduate Excellence Scholarship 2027 requires an application through the University of Reading applicant portal. Applicants must have an eligible course application and academic offer. Additional supporting documents: not specified.
