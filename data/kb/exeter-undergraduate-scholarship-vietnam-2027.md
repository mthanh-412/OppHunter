# University of Exeter Undergraduate Scholarship for Vietnam 2027/28

## Key facts
University of Exeter Undergraduate Scholarship for Vietnam 2027/28 is offered by the University of Exeter to eligible Vietnamese students. Level: undergraduate, new Year 1 entrants. Funding: GBP 3,000 recurring tuition-fee discount for each eligible year of study. Application deadline: 2027-06-30. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.exeter.ac.uk/study/funding/award/?id=5988.

## Eligibility
University of Exeter Undergraduate Scholarship for Vietnam 2027/28 is for Vietnamese citizens who pay international fees, are self-funded and hold an offer for an eligible campus-based undergraduate programme starting in September 2027. Applicants must enter directly into Year 1. Eligible subject areas include Economics, Finance and Accounting, Management and related fields. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course entry requirements apply.

## Required documents
University of Exeter Undergraduate Scholarship for Vietnam 2027/28 does not require a separate scholarship application according to the award information. Applicants need to submit an undergraduate course application and receive an eligible offer. Other documents follow the course admission requirements.
