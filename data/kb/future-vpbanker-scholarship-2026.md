# Future VPBanker Scholarship 2026

## Key facts
Future VPBanker Scholarship 2026 is offered by Vietnam Prosperity Joint Stock Commercial Bank (VPBank) to university students. Level: undergraduate, mainly Year 3 and final-year students. Funding: cash award of up to VND 20 million, potential internship opportunities, an employer-brand ambassador role and access to the VPBank Young Talents final interview. Application deadline: 2026-10-11. Status: closed. Official page: 
https://hr.vpbank.com.vn/ta/FutureVPBanker/index.html.

## Eligibility
Future VPBanker Scholarship 2026 targets Year 3 or final-year students in Economics, Finance, Banking, Insurance, Business Administration, Law, Marketing, Information Technology, Data Science or related fields. Minimum GPA: 8.0/10. English certificates, leadership and extracurricular involvement are preferred; a mandatory IELTS score is not specified. Applicants must not have received a VPBank scholarship in 2024–2025.

## Required documents
Future VPBanker Scholarship 2026 requires an online application. The official page does not provide a complete supporting-document list; additional evidence should be checked in the application portal.
