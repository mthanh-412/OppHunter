# International Excellence Scholarship (January/March 2027 entry)

## Key facts
International Excellence Scholarship (January/March 2027 entry) is offered by Swansea University to eligible international students. Level: undergraduate and postgraduate taught. Funding: tuition-fee reduction of up to GBP 5,000 in Year 1; eligible undergraduate recipients may receive an additional GBP 2,000 in Year 2 if they achieve at least a 70% average. Application deadline: 2026-11-27. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.swansea.ac.uk/international-students/my-finances/international-scholarships/international-excellence-scholarships/.

## Eligibility
International Excellence Scholarship (January/March 2027 entry) requires an international, self-funded applicant to hold an offer for an eligible course and use the application route for their country of ordinary residence. Undergraduate and postgraduate taught applicants may be eligible. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
International Excellence Scholarship (January/March 2027 entry) requires the scholarship application form and academic transcripts. Applicants whose transcripts do not show an official average or GPA may need to provide confirmation from their previous institution. Other documents depend on the application route.
