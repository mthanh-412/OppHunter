# International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027 is listed by the University of Sheffield for eligible international undergraduate students. Level: undergraduate. Funding: GBP 2,500 per year, subject to maintaining at least a 60% average, with a maximum of GBP 10,000 for a four-year programme. Application process: applicants need an eligible undergraduate course application and an offer; whether a separate scholarship application is required is not specified. GPA and IELTS requirements: scholarship-specific thresholds are not specified; course admission requirements apply. Application deadline: not specified. Status: not specified. Official page: 
https://sheffield.ac.uk/international/fees-and-funding/scholarships/undergraduate.
