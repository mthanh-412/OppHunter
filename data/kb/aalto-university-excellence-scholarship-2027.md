# Aalto University Excellence Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Aalto University Excellence Scholarship 2027 is offered by Aalto University to high-achieving fee-paying students in eligible English-taught programmes. Level: bachelor's and master's. Funding: 100% tuition-fee waiver for the target duration of the degree; living costs are not covered. Application deadline: 2027-01-22. Status: open. Official page:
https://www.aalto.fi/en/admission-services/scholarships-and-tuition-fees.

## Eligibility
Aalto University Excellence Scholarship 2027 is for non-EU/EEA/Swiss applicants who are liable to pay tuition fees and apply to an eligible programme. Selection is merit-based and highly competitive. GPA and English-language requirements depend on the programme and admission route; a scholarship-wide IELTS threshold is not specified.

## Required documents
Aalto University Excellence Scholarship 2027 requires applicants to request the scholarship as part of the programme admission application. Academic certificates and transcripts, proof of language proficiency and programme-specific admission documents are required according to the selected programme.
