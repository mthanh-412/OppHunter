# Undergraduate International Excellence Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Undergraduate International Excellence Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of Leeds to international undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate, new Year 1 entrants. Funding: tuition-fee discounts of GBP 3,000, GBP 6,000 or GBP 16,000 in the first year. Application deadline: 2027-04-11. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.leeds.ac.uk/scholarship/15/undergraduate-international-excellence-scholarships.

## Eligibility
Undergraduate International Excellence Scholarship 2027 is for international-fee students with a conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible September 2027 undergraduate course. Applicants must be self-funded or partially funded and enter Year 1. Strong academic performance and extracurricular contribution are considered. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course requirements apply.

## Required documents
Undergraduate International Excellence Scholarship 2027 requires an online scholarship application and an eligible University of Leeds course application/offer. Academic records and evidence of extracurricular activities should be supplied as requested in the application.
