# Reach Oxford Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Reach Oxford Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of Oxford to students from eligible low-income countries. Level: undergraduate. Funding: course fees, an annual living-cost grant and one return air fare per year, normally for three or four years. Application deadline: 2027-01-26. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/fees-and-funding/oxford-bursaries-and-scholarships/reach-oxford.

## Eligibility
Reach Oxford Scholarship 2027 is for applicants from eligible countries, including Vietnam, who receive an offer for an Oxford undergraduate course. Applicants should demonstrate exceptional academic ability, financial need and social commitment. Priority is given to those who have not previously studied at undergraduate level. Medicine is excluded. GPA and IELTS scholarship thresholds: not specified; Oxford course requirements apply.

## Required documents
Reach Oxford Scholarship 2027 requires an Oxford undergraduate application through UCAS and an offer of admission. Eligible offer-holders must submit the separate scholarship application when the 2027 form becomes available, including information about financial need and personal impact.
