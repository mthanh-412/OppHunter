# Think Big Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Think Big Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of Bristol to international students. Level: undergraduate and postgraduate taught. Funding: undergraduate tuition-fee awards of GBP 3,000, GBP 6,500 or GBP 13,000 per year for up to four years, subject to the award terms. Application deadline: 2027-03-19. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/support/finances/scholarships/think-big-scholarships/.

## Eligibility
Think Big Scholarship 2027 is for international/overseas-fee applicants who have applied for an eligible in-person course starting in September 2027. An offer is not required before submitting the scholarship application. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course admission requirements apply.

## Required documents
Think Big Scholarship 2027 requires a University of Bristol course application and application ID, followed by the online international scholarship application. Supporting evidence must be provided as requested in the application.
