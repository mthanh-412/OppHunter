# TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship 2027 is provided by the Taiwan International Cooperation and Development Fund. Level: bachelor's, master's or PhD, depending on the participating programme. Funding: full scholarship package; exact coverage depends on the university and programme. Application deadline: 2027-03-15. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.icdf.org.tw/wSite/ct?ctNode=31280&mp=2&xItem=12505.

## Eligibility
TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship 2027 is for citizens of eligible TaiwanICDF partner countries applying to listed programmes at participating Taiwanese universities. Degree level, GPA and language requirements vary by programme. The detailed 2027 guidebook and programme-specific requirements should be checked when published.

## Required documents
TaiwanICDF International Higher Education Scholarship 2027 requires an online application and the academic, recommendation and identity documents specified in the annual guidebook and selected university's requirements. The full 2027 document checklist: not specified at the verification date.
