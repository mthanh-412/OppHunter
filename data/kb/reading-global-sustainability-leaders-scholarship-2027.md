# Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarship 2027

## Key facts
Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarship 2027 is offered by the University of Reading to international undergraduate applicants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: GBP 6,000 per year for up to four years towards living costs, together with a scholarship development scheme. Application deadline: 2027-01-18. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.reading.ac.uk/ready-to-study/study/fees-and-funding/scholarships/international-scholarships.

## Eligibility
Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarship 2027 is intended for international undergraduate applicants who meet the University's eligible-course and admission conditions. GPA, IELTS and country-specific requirements: not specified on the linked scholarship overview; applicants should confirm the detailed award terms.

## Required documents
Global Sustainability Leaders Scholarship 2027 requires an undergraduate application and the relevant scholarship application materials through the University of Reading process. A complete list of supporting documents: not specified on the linked overview.
