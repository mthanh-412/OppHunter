# York International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027/28

## Key facts
York International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027/28 is offered by the University of York to eligible international undergraduate entrants. Level: undergraduate. Funding: GBP 5,000 per year of study, automatically awarded to eligible applicants. Application deadline: 2027-07-01. Status: open. Official page: 
https://www.york.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/fees-funding/international/scholarships/york-international-ug-scholarship/.

## Eligibility
York International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027/28 includes applicants from Vietnam. Applicants must pay overseas fees, hold a conditional or unconditional offer by 1 July 2027 and enrol in a full-time, campus-based undergraduate course starting in September 2027. Fully sponsored students are not eligible. Scholarship-specific GPA and IELTS thresholds: not specified; course entry requirements apply.

## Required documents
York International Undergraduate Scholarship 2027/28 is automatically assessed, so no separate scholarship application is required. Applicants need an eligible undergraduate course application and offer by the stated date. Other documents follow the course admission process.
